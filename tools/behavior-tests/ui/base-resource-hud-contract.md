# Visible base resources and energy allocation

`ui.base_resource_hud.v1` records two independent fresh original executions per
case. The fixture and Enter/Escape/B sequences match the declared one-Doctor
Headquarters in [the base-opening contract](base-open-recalc-contract.md).
No income turn advances and allocation controls are not changed.

Each first opening and reopening observes the visible resource intake,
consumption and net result for nutrients, minerals and energy, their displayed
three-digit labels, the overall surplus/shortfall label, and Economy/Labs/Psych
percentage, energy, bonus and total. Native population and base identity are
guarded. OCR uses three scales, consensus, and O-to-zero normalization only in
numeric regions. The observer waits for complete readable resource/allocation
panels: the base title can appear before the upper window finishes opening.
Unreadable or incomplete frames never become inferred answers.

The original control displays nutrient balance zero and SURPLUS. The joined
city displays nutrient shortfall and SHORTFALL. Both display allocation
50/50/0; the visible Doctor Psych bonus changes from two to four. These are
observations of the declared initial Gaian city, not general allocation,
rounding, support or economic rules. The signed native nutrient counter is
excluded and remains in its separate strict pending contract.

The reproduction adapter executes actual production selection, joining,
management preparation, popup opening and closing, native intake/consumption,
the shipped resource callback, and resource/energy label updates. Its synthetic
four-wide geometry is equivalent only for this unworked Headquarters fixture;
it does not establish general terrain or worker allocation parity. Display
nodes, frontend transport and icon-cell drawing are boundaries. A read-only
production refresh mounts the resource panel into the actual test popup body.
Values are parsed from displayed labels, not calculated from expected answers.

Leading padding in allocation numbers is ignored. Resource-result equals
prefixes, signs and three-digit formatting remain observed properties. A
missing prefix or wrong status remains a mismatch. Other factions, policy
choices, supported units, workers, terrain, income turns, allocation editing,
resource icon layout and arbitrary energy rounding remain unobserved.
