`spread` fixture: one healthy, ready Former on the free southeast tile, with
Scout and Colony Pod companions and one owned city. Two synthetic installed
source profiles supply distinct common `DISBAND2` wording and conflicting
expansion declarations.

at all 17 checkpoints. They cover Escape and default confirmation cancellation,
reopening with Shift+D, arrow selection and ordinary retirement through Enter
or the real OK button. The root menu stays closed throughout. Confirmation
removes only the Former and selects the Colony Pod; city state, paused year,
surviving membership, positions, health, orders and movement are guarded.

root/child visibility, native selected role and ordered surviving unit records.

read their real native effects; substituting Action-menu clicks or constructing
dialog/selection answers from the input sequence is outside the contract.

This contract excludes city recycling, overload effects, other initial orders,
alternate modifiers, movement exhaustion, other unit kinds, general keyboard
shortcut behavior and AI parity. Additional application regressions may check
controller authority and lifetime without counting them as original evidence.
