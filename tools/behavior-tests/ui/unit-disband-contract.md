# Ready Former disband controls

`ui.unit_disband.v1` covers one healthy, unspent Former on a flat arid free tile
one southeast step from an owned city. A ready Scout remains at that city and a
ready Colony Pod one southwest step away. Each engine prepares the declared
`spread` fixture before the first checkpoint; the original uses real movement,
Hold and turn controls from the guarded original-created starting save.
`initial_base` describes the city before preparation. Preparation income and
raw city stores are guarded within each engine, rather than equated between
engines.

Both traces press Tab, open MENU and Action, and click source slot nine. They
cancel and reopen before confirming ordinary disband. The first uses Escape
and then Down/Enter. The second confirms the untouched default before reopening,
visits the available choice highlights with arrows, and uses the actual OK
button. Its third choice is visited only as a draft; self-destruct activation
and its effects are outside this contract.

Three public profiles declare the Action row and common `Script.txt` `DISBAND2`
caption, body and three choices, together with conflicting expansion declarations.
All recorded profiles display the common declaration, establishing precedence
for this bounded dialog.
The supplementary name profile declares `Retire $UNITTYPE0?`, opens the dialog
and cancels. The original displays `RETIRE FORMERS?` for the selected Former.
This observes the unit-name substitution for that token and unit only. The two
main traces and their accepted original evidence remain unchanged.

The output contains independently read dialog caption/body/choices/highlight/OK,
menu visibility, native selection, owned city count, surviving unit roles/order
codes and movement in thirds of a movement point. Display text is uppercase
with folded whitespace. No source body or action supplies an observed answer.

The observer cross-checks the visible selected-unit name against native state,
requires a paused year and guards city membership, identity, citizen records and
stores. It checks every surviving role, position, health, order and movement.
Native unit indices remain guarded before deletion and are read independently
afterwards; no cross-engine identity numbering or persistent original array-index
policy is assumed. Every checkpoint requires two identical full observations.
Only immutable records from two independent complete original runs are eligible
as reference evidence. Original binaries, screenshots, saves and research remain
outside the repository.

Candidate replay must invoke the actual mounted Action row and dialog controls,
read native unit/city state and use real production commands for any removal.
The adapter cannot assign choices, default selection, menu lifetime, command
success or surviving membership. The scope excludes other unit types, initial
orders, movement already spent, active terraforming, city recycling, damage,
self-destruct activation, other source layouts, later turns and complete
original disband or AI behavior.
