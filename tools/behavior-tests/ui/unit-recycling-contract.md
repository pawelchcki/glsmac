# Owned-city Disband controls and recycling

`ui.unit_recycling.v1` exercises one healthy unspent Scout on its owned city's
tile, with a ready Former one southeast step away and Colony Pod one southwest
step away. Each engine prepares the existing `spread` fixture before the first
checkpoint. The original uses actual controls from its guarded original-created
save. The declared `initial_base` describes the city before that preparation.

A separate stacked fixture leaves all three units on the owned city. Its trace
presses Tab to select the Former and uses the synthetic Scout declaration with
the selected native unit name. Its accepted evidence is stored separately from
the two unchanged Scout cases.

Two synthetic profiles declare distinct Action-source9 labels, common `DISBAND`
caption/body/two-choice text, a separate three-choice `DISBAND2` declaration and
conflicting expansion text. Both traces cancel or confirm the initial default,
reopen, navigate the draft and confirm the selected retirement choice. The second
also presses Down at the last choice before using the actual OK button.

In the accepted observations both owned-city units display the two-choice common
`DISBAND` declaration. Ordinary confirmation removes the selected Scout or Former,
selects the next ready unit, and changes stored minerals by four or eight
respectively. These observations are confined to the declared starting recipes
and saved-game context; they do not establish a general refund formula.

Each checkpoint independently reads actual dialog text, choice count/highlight,
button, root/child menu visibility, native selected role and surviving unit
membership/orders/movement. `mineral_delta` is the native city's stored minerals
minus its own engine's prepared baseline. This separates the command effect
from income incurred during fixture preparation; raw baseline stores are not
asserted equal across engines. Native city identity, population, citizen records,
non-mineral stores, membership, paused year and each surviving unit's role,
position, health, orders and movement remain guarded within the original.

Only immutable observations from fresh complete repeated original runs can be
accepted. The candidate must invoke mounted production controls and native
commands and read their actual state. Source declarations and input actions
cannot supply observed choices, success, selection or mineral changes.

The scope excludes other unit costs, industry ratings, faction modifiers,
damaged/spent units, other cities, overload effects and complete disband,
recycling, economy or AI behavior.
